## Scope

Defines the public-safe decision/control family governing material decisions in **SSR & Context Assembly**. This specification identifies decision responsibilities and evidence expectations, not private implementation tolerances.

## Governed decisions

- Are required protected sections available?
- Is an optional section eligible?
- Does the optional section fit protected budget?
- Are omission/redaction/provenance reasons recorded?
- If bounded Senate is enabled, did advisory work complete within deadline; otherwise degrade to baseline without transferring authority?

## Required evidence direction

Where the target runtime makes a governed PASS/FAIL or equivalent disposition, the evidence model should be sufficient to identify the decision result, applicable receipt/evidence references, trusted turn/correlation identity, time, and the governing control revision.

## Claim ceiling

Exact SSR ordering, ranking, prompt text, thresholds and private section-selection logic are withheld. Runtime receipt binding to an approved CTRL-400 revision remains a V5 requirement.

## Withheld

Exact thresholds, weights, policy expressions, SQL, prompt/preprompt text, credentials, sensitive schemas and proprietary selection/activation logic remain private.
